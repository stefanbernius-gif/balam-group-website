# Balam Group Real Estate

## Overview
Premium real estate platform connecting American expats and international buyers with property opportunities in Mérida, Yucatán. Guided buying process from webinar to closing.

## Users
- American expats considering relocation to Mexico
- International property investors (primarily US-based)
- Age 50–70, mid-high income, seeking lifestyle upgrade or investment

## Brand Tone
Confident, transparent, educational. Authority without gatekeeping. Gregory's voice: pragmatic American who "made the move and never looked back."

## Product Purpose
Demystify foreign property buying. Build trust through education, social proof (500+ closings), and expert guidance. Convert curious visitors → webinar attendees → property explorers → buyers.

brand

## Strategic Principles
- Trust through transparency: no hidden fees, no sales pressure
- Social proof: 500+ closings, testimonials, team credentials
- Education first: webinar, FAQ, fideicomiso explainer
- Lifestyle angle: sell the dream (morning swims, sunsets, peace), not just the property
- American expertise in Mexican market
