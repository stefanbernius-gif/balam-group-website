Your Google Ads campaign targets people at the exact moment they're searching for what you offer. Instead of hoping people find you, you appear in their search results when they type specific keywords into Google.

What Happens When Someone Clicks Your Ad

  1. Someone types a keyword — e.g., "fideicomiso Mexico" or "buy property Mérida"
  2. Your ad appears — At the top of Google search results with your headline and description
  3. They click — They land on your website (already optimized for mobile)
  4. They engage — They see your trust signals (500+ families guided), read about fideicomiso, and either:
    • Click "Chat Now" → WhatsApp conversation with you
    • Fill out contact form → You follow up by phone/email
    • Browse properties → Explore your listings
